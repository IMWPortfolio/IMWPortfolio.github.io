About

Artist Statement

My work in Digital Media Art explores the feeling of making something that doesn’t exist, wondering if I exist, deciding I probably do, and pondering the absurdity of all existence. I am particularly interested in unearthing ancient DMA tools and techniques and using them to create pieces that either make someone smile or make them uncomfortable or make them smile because they’re uncomfortable. Ultimately, I want viewers of my work to question whether or not I should be doing this at all.
